## Major Art Projects

### 747 Big Imagination Foundation (2016-2020)
**Burning Man, Nevada, USA**
- **Role**: Co-leader
- **Medium**: Refitted Boeing 747, over 100,000 individually addressable LEDs, interactive systems, sound installation
- **Achievement**: Largest mobile artwork ever at Burning Man
- **Scale**: Required hundreds of volunteers over 4 years, large-scale fundraising, and extensive physical labor
- **Features**: Interactive experience, sound installation, 3D lighting display
- **Future**: Permanent display at Las Vegas Area 15 (2025)

### A Symphony of Circuit and Soil (2025)
**SFER IK Museum, Tulum, Mexico** | *with Mika Revell*
- **Recognition**: Winner of Art in Nature Award at Art Basel Miami 2024
- **Contribution**: Technical design, AI system architecture, machine vision integration
