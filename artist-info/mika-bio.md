# Mika Lauren Revell - Biography

Mika Lauren Revell is an accomplished artist working at the intersection of science and art. Collaborating with Alex Finnemore, she creates works that explore how biology can inform sustainable material design and natural design language in the contemporary art world. Her practice spans large-scale public sculptures, immersive experiences, and installations across multiple countries.

Revell holds a BFA in Fine Art from Otis College of Art and Design, an MFA in Sculpture from Central Saint Martins, and an MA in Psychology from Pepperdine University. This interdisciplinary background informs her unique approach to art-making that bridges scientific inquiry with aesthetic exploration.

## Artist Statement

My art practice investigates the relationship between natural processes and human intervention. Through collaborative projects with scientists and engineers, I create works that reveal the hidden patterns and intelligence within natural systems. By employing both traditional craft techniques and advanced technologies, my sculptures and installations invite viewers to reconsider their relationship with the natural world.

I am particularly interested in biomimicry—the practice of looking to nature for solutions to human challenges. My work often incorporates sustainable materials and processes inspired by biological systems, from the layered structure of nacre (mother-of-pearl) to the complex networks of mycelium. These natural phenomena inform both the aesthetic and functional aspects of my sculptures.

Through my collaborative practice with physicist Alex Finnemore, we create works that exist at the nexus of art, science, and environmental consciousness. Our installations aim to make visible the invisible forces that shape our world, whether they be geological processes, ecological relationships, or the mathematical principles underlying natural forms.
