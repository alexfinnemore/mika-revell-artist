## Public Commissions

### A Symphony of Circuit and Soil (2025)
**SFER IK Museum, Tulum, Mexico** | *Art in Nature Award Winner*
- **Medium**: Rammed-earth sculpture, reflective curved void, AI-generated soundscape, machine vision
- **Concept**: Earthwork installation mimicking sedimentary rock with reflective curved void, inspired by indigenous practices. AI-generated soundscape combines field-recorded animal calls with synthetic meditative tones, responding to visitor presence through machine vision tracking.
- **Collaborators**: Mika Revell, Alex Finnemore, Graeme Revell (sound)
- **Support**: Azulik Fab Lab funding
- **Budget**: $80,000

## Proposed/Shortlisted Works

### To Weave the Sky
**L+A+N+D Bernheim Forest (Shortlisted), Kamiyama AIR (Proposed)**
- **Medium**: Suspended, modular rope installation
- **Concept**: Invites visitors to move through forest canopy with hands and feet. Inspired by mycelial networks and tree-dwelling animal movement.
