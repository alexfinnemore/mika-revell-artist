## Strategic Insights

### What Works
1. Deep research into site ecology and cultural context
2. Clear technical feasibility with detailed specifications
3. AI/technology that enhances rather than dominates the natural experience
4. Materials that connect to place
5. Interactive elements that create dialogue with visitors

### Areas to Explore
- More varied material palettes beyond rope and earth
- Different scales of interactivity
- Stronger integration of Alex's biomimicry research
- Sound as primary medium (building on SFER IK success)
