# Living Matter Studio - Artist Statement

*Mika Revell + Alex Finnemore*

---

## About

Living Matter Studio is a collaborative practice founded by artist Mika Revell and physicist Alex Finnemore. We create large-scale sculptural installations that explore the intersection of science, art, and environmental consciousness.

Our work addresses sustainable material design, natural design language, and contemporary art applications. Productions span eight countries including large-scale public sculptures and immersive experiences.

## Our Approach

We believe that art can reveal the hidden intelligence of natural systems. Our installations make visible the invisible forces that shape our world—geological processes, ecological relationships, and the mathematical principles underlying natural forms.

Each project begins with deep research into the site, its ecology, its cultural history, and its materials. We work with local craftspeople and scientists to develop works that are both technically innovative and deeply rooted in place.

## Core Themes

**Biomimicry**
Looking to nature for design solutions. From the layered structure of nacre to the branching patterns of mycelium, natural systems offer blueprints for sustainable, resilient structures.

**Material Inquiry**
Exploring the expressive potential of materials: rammed earth, dichroic glass, rope, natural fibers. Each material carries meaning and connects to place.

**Interactivity**
Creating responsive environments that change with visitor presence, time of day, and seasonal cycles. Our installations are not static objects but dynamic systems.

**Site-Responsiveness**
Deep connection to place, culture, and local materials. We don't impose art on a site—we grow it from the site's own possibilities.

**Science-Art Intersection**
Alex's research in biomimetic materials and Mika's sculptural practice combine to create work that is both scientifically informed and aesthetically compelling.
