## Collaborative Partnership: Revell + Finnemore

*Working at the intersection of science and art*

Collaborative team combining expertise in natural sciences with conceptual and visual arts. Work addresses sustainable material design, natural design language, and contemporary art applications. Productions span eight countries including large-scale public sculptures and immersive experiences.

### Recent Collaborative Works

**2025** | *A Symphony of Circuit and Soil*
- **Location**: SFER IK Museum, Tulum, Mexico
- **Medium**: Rammed Earth Cube, Hyperbolic Void, Generative Audio Installation
- **Recognition**: Winner of Art in Nature Award at Art Basel Miami 2024
- **Description**: Earthwork installation incorporating AI-generated audio derived from Yucatán Peninsula ecology. Features rammed-earth sculpture modeled after sedimentary rock, enclosing reflective hyperbolic void. AI soundscape weaves field-recorded animal calls with synthetic meditative tones, dynamically responding to visitor presence through machine vision tracking.
- **Support**: Funded by Azulik Fab Lab

**Proposed** | *To Weave the Sky*
- **Status**: Shortlisted for L+A+N+D Bernheim Forest and Arboretum, Research Forest 2026
- **Medium**: Suspended, modular rope installation
- **Concept**: Invites forest canopy movement through hands and feet, offering immersive natural world encounter. Inspired by mycelial networks and tree-dwelling animal movement.

**Proposed** | *Les Mondes Emboîtés*
- **Medium**: Dichroic Glass Modules
- **Concept**: Contemporary counterpart to historical garden grottoes and follies. Archway form inspired by Normandy cliffs, using iridescent dichroic glass with shifting colors and reflectivity based on sunlight angle and viewer position.
- **Construction**: Modular units adaptable as freestanding elements, suspended structures, or integrated garden architecture.
