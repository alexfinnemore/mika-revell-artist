# Reflection: To Weave the Sky (KAIR 2025)

## Outcome

- **Status**: Did not win
- **Year**: 2025 application cycle

---

## Possible Factors

- First submission to KAIR
- Concept may have been too similar to other forest rope installations
- Unknown competition level
- May not have demonstrated enough site-specific connection to Kamiyama

---

## Lessons to Consider

1. Research past KAIR winners more deeply to understand what resonates
2. Develop stronger connection to specific Kamiyama context
3. Consider how to differentiate from standard "rope in forest" installations
4. Explore integration of technology (building on SFER IK success)
5. Emphasize Mika's prior Japan residency (AIR 3331, 2016)

---

## Reusable Elements

- Core concept of Voronoi-patterned canopy installation
- Mycelial network inspiration
- Participatory body-in-forest experience
- Modular rope system

---

## Questions for KAIR 2026

- What makes this application different from 2025?
- How to connect more deeply to Kamiyama specifically?
- Should we incorporate sound/AI elements (like SFER IK)?
- How to leverage Japan residency experience?
