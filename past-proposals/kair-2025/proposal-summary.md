## Concept Summary

A large-scale woven installation suspended in the forest canopy, inviting visitors to move through the space using hands and feet. The work draws inspiration from:

- **Mycelial networks**: Underground fungal connections between trees
- **Tree-dwelling animal movement**: Pathways through the canopy
- **Voronoi geometry**: Patterns found in insect wings, giraffe coats, muscle fibers

The installation shifts perception to place the human body within the forest's living structure, offering an immersive natural world encounter.
