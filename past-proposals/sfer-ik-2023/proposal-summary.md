## Summary

A soundscape sculpture that uses AI to interact with the audience and promote reflection and connection with nature. The rammed-earth sculpture features a reflective curved void in the shape of a hyperboloid, inspired by indigenous ways of being and designed to challenge anthropocentrism.

The AI-generated soundscape (named "The Chorus") combines field-recorded animal calls with synthetic meditative tones and responds to the presence and energy level of visitors. The installation creates a semi-subterranean space where visitors can experience the interconnectedness of humans and nature through both physical structure and interactive sound.

The sculpture evokes journeys through deep memories, beginning with an approach through dense jungle, triggered by rumbling sounds. Visitors are invited to explore the installation from multiple perspectives - entering the pit, circumnavigating the artwork, viewing it from afar, and inspecting the hundreds of layers of materials in the wall.

---

## Context and Purpose

This proposal responds to themes of Ancestral Knowledge & Technology, Interspecies Art, and Environmental Symbiosis. It challenges anthropocentric perspectives and reconnects humans with nature through:

1. Examining common themes across indigenous cultural practices
2. Looking to pre-human ancestors for wisdom about living in harmony with nature
3. Exploring the paradoxical role of AI in both environmental destruction and potential healing
4. Creating a physical space for reflection and interconnection

The artwork re-situates manufactured materials against literal earth, rejecting Eurocentric artistic perspectives and approaching knowledge from a holistic perspective that includes all living beings.

## Technical Specifications

### Construction
- Excavation: 3m x 6m rectangular space, 0.5m depth
- Foundation: Concrete slab, 3m x 3m
- Wall construction: Rammed earth, 0.5m thick
- Materials: Gravel, sand, clay, cement, recycled materials

### Systems
- Audio: Dolby Atmos system
- AI: Custom software for generative soundscape
- Machine vision: Visitor tracking
- Power: Electrical connection (optional solar panels)
- Lighting: Ambient lighting for evening viewing
