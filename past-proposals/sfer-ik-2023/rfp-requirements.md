# SFER IK Art in Nature Award - Requirements

## Overview

The SFER IK Award focuses on art that engages with themes of:
- Ancestral Knowledge & Technology
- Interspecies Art
- Environmental Symbiosis

## Location Context

- Located in the Yucatán, Mexico
- Set in a natural jungle environment
- SFER IK exists "outside the institutional confines of 'the museum'"
- Provides opportunity to "reintroduce man to the wild"

## Budget

- $80,000 production budget

## Key Focus Areas

Based on the call, the RFP emphasized:
1. Engagement with indigenous/ancestral knowledge
2. Connection to natural environment
3. Innovation in art forms and materials
4. Sustainability and environmental consciousness
5. Interactive elements for visitor engagement

## Recognition

- Winner announced at Art Basel Miami
- Work installed at SFER IK Museum, Tulum
