## Initial Directions

### "To Weave the Sky" (Adapted)
Previously proposed for Bernheim Forest. Could adapt for Kamiyama context:
- Large-scale woven installation using rope in forest canopy
- Voronoi geometry patterns (found in insect wings, giraffe coats, muscle fibers)
- Connection to Shinto traditions of sacred rope (shimenawa)
- Hinoki cypress or sugi cedar forest setting

### Questions to Explore
- What makes Kamiyama unique vs other forest residencies?
- How do local craft traditions (indigo dyeing, woodwork) inform the work?
- What is the relationship to Shinto sacred sites in the area?
- How does community engagement work during the 69-day residency?
