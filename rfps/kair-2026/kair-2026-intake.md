## ELIGIBILITY & RESTRICTIONS

**Eligible disciplines:**
- Painting, drawing, sculpture, installation, land art
- NOT eligible: production art

**Requirements:**
- Work independently and manage daily life
- Produce at least one artwork during residency
- Present 4/5 of program period (minimum 40 days before exhibition)
- Participate in community life and exchange programs
- Speak and understand conversational English
- Good health
- Own travel insurance required

**Important notes:**
- Limited resources - not comparable to museums/galleries
- Artist responsible for time management and completion
- If third-party professional assistance needed, must indicate in application
- Guests (spouse, partner, assistant, child) require prior approval and additional fee

## STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

### Unique Opportunity Aspects

1. **"God's Mountain" philosophy** - Deep connection to place, nature, and community. They explicitly want artists influenced by "timeless nature" and "interaction with friendly local people"

2. **Process-focused residency** - 69 days with community integration (workshops, food exchange, open studio). Values the journey, not just the final work

3. **Limited resources = authenticity** - They explicitly state they can't match museum resources. This favors artists who work with local/found materials and site-responsive approaches

### Potential Challenges

1. **Work ownership** - Artwork becomes KAIR property (though negotiable). Need to consider if this affects the proposal

2. **Solo application only** - Form is for individual artist. Need to clarify if Living Matter Studio can apply as collaborative duo, or if Mika applies solo

3. **Previous application** - Applied in 2025 and didn't win. Need to differentiate this application significantly

- Process-based: 69-day making period
