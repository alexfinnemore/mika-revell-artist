# Themes Update

Analyze all past proposals to identify patterns and update the themes database.

## Instructions

1. **Gather Data**
   - Read all proposals in `/past-proposals/`
   - Extract from each:
     - Themes and concepts used
     - Technical approaches and materials
     - Budget range
     - Outcome (won/lost/finalist)
     - Year submitted

2. **Thematic Analysis**
   - Categorize recurring themes
   - Identify emerging themes (last 2 years)
   - Map theme evolution over time
   - Note themes that correlate with success

3. **Success Pattern Analysis**
   - Compare winning vs non-winning proposals:
     - Common themes and approaches
     - Budget ranges
     - Technical specifications
     - Collaboration patterns
   - Calculate success rates by category

4. **Technical Approach Mapping**
   - Categorize by primary materials/techniques
   - Track technology integration methods
   - Note most/least successful approaches

5. **Strategic Gap Analysis**
   - Identify underexplored themes or techniques
   - Compare portfolio against current public art trends
   - Suggest potential new directions

6. **Update Database**
   - Rewrite `/artist-info/themes-database.md` with:
     - Updated theme categorizations
     - Success metrics and patterns
     - Strategic recommendations

7. **Deliver Summary**
   - Top 3 themes/approaches to emphasize
   - Collaboration strategies with highest success potential
   - Technical approaches to explore or avoid

## Output Location

`/artist-info/themes-database.md`
