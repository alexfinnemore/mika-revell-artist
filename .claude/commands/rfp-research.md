# RFP Research

Conduct deep research on the institution, site, and competitive landscape for Living Matter Studio proposals.

## Instructions

1. **Read Intake Document**
   - Load `/rfps/[institution]-[year]/[institution]-[year]-intake.md`
   - Note key details: institution, location, deadline, themes

2. **Research Areas** (cover all five):

   **INSTITUTION ANALYSIS**
   - Mission, history, and strategic priorities
   - Previous public art initiatives and budgets
   - Key stakeholders, board members, funding sources
   - Public reputation and recent news
   - Current organizational priorities

   **DECISION-MAKER PROFILES** (if available)
   - Selection committee member backgrounds
   - Past projects they've championed
   - Published writings, interviews, statements
   - Artistic preferences

   **SITE & CONTEXT RESEARCH**
   - Location history and cultural significance
   - Community demographics and engagement patterns
   - Environmental conditions (climate, terrain, ecology)
   - Local materials and craft traditions
   - Accessibility and visibility factors
   - Future development plans

   **COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE**
   - Previous winning proposals (themes, approaches)
   - Success patterns and common characteristics
   - Current public art trends relevant to this opportunity
   - Living Matter's distinctive advantages

   **STRATEGIC INTELLIGENCE**
   - Potential collaborative partners
   - Community concerns or political considerations
   - Media attention opportunities
   - Connection points to Living Matter themes

3. **Identify Alignment**
   - Map opportunity to Living Matter's core themes:
     - Biomimicry
     - Material inquiry
     - Interactivity
     - Site-responsiveness
     - Science-art intersection
   - Note Mika's relevant prior experience (especially Japan residency 2016)

4. **Create Output File**
   - Save as `[institution]-[year]-research.md` (e.g., `kair-2026-research.md`)
   - Include source links for all findings
   - Highlight 3 key insights that should guide the proposal

## Output Location

`/rfps/[institution]-[year]/[institution]-[year]-research.md`
